# HowTo: Ethics and Legals in Photography

This introduction to photography ethics and legals serves as a quick start for beginners. 
It is by no means designed to replace legal advisory!

> **No legal council was part in the conception of the below text.**
> If you are in a different part of the world, please take extra caution and double-check. 
> Furthermore, perspectives on right-and-wrong change over time.
> Use with caution, and make up your own mind in a situation-to-situation basis.
> No guarantees granted.

This is not meant to be a complete guide. 
The focus here lies with understanding basic concepts within the German legal system and cultural perceptions of photography. 
Please refer to named sources for more information or start your own research.
In doubt, consult an expert/layer! 

Feedback and error correction always welcome.

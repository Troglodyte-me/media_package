# HowTo: Ethics and Legals in Photography

This introduction to photography ethics and legals serves as a quick start for beginners. 
It is by now means designed to replace legal advisory. 

> **No legal council was part in the conception of the below text.**
> Use with caution, and make up your own mind in a situation-to-situation basis.

Furthermore, it's not meant to be a complete guide. 
The focus here lies with the German legal system and cultural perception of photography. 
Please refer to named sources for more information or start your own research.

Feedback and error correction always welcome.

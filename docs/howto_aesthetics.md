# Aesthetics
The beauty of an image -- as a matter of taste -- lies often within the eye of the viewer. 
Aesthetics in photography is the art to produce an image capable of triggering a certain emotion or thought. 

Many aspects of image aesthetics, can be altered in [post-production](.\howto_post.en.md). 
However, in regards to overall quality, it is favoured to cover most (if not all) aspects of aesthetics already when shooting the picture -- as far as it is technically possible. 
Yet, some aspects of aesthetics remain a matter of alteration for example when adding or subtracting elements of an photo or compiling a complete new image.

In either way, is aesthetics a framework with tools to analyze as well as reproduce art with meaning.


This introduction to the topic serves as a quick start for beginners. 
It focuses on giving a handy set of rules and interpretations for everyday situations.
It's not meant to be a complete guide. 
As aesthetics are a vast field knowledge, interpretation and discussion, this guide can only be a first start.
Please refer to named sources for more information or start your own research.

Feedback and error correction always welcome.

## Image Composition
### Rules of ...
Most famously the "Rule-of-Thirds" is used to composite interesting photos and is probably the most easy and useful advice to give. 
> If not already displayed in your camera, divide mentally the image in three sections of equal size vertically as well as horizontally, giving you a framework of nine segments.
> Try to position subjects of interest on an intersection of horizontal and vertical lines. 
> When the subject is long (like the horizon) or high (like a building) try to align them with one of the respective horizontal/vertical lines. 

### Ma (negative space)
Originally a traditional Japanese art style, that utilizing "empty" space to emote reflection in the viewer.

## Facts establishing
When claiming truth, one is either relyant on their own experience and proof (like photos), or some external verification. 
This is true for science, journalism and also public relation (incl. social media). 

As a general rule of thumb, a claim can be establish as factual if at least (but not limited to) two independent sources establish the same finding. 
Be aware, that both sources should be primary sources like witnesses, research of dedicated scientists or actual evidence.
In contrast secondary sources are only quoting the (original) discovery and are therefore prone to error or quote one-another without actual proof. Those are to be avoided and - if no other is available - scrutinised before publication.
